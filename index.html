How a game becomes a picture

The canvas is the board

White sits at the bottom and Black at the top, so wherever a style draws, it draws where the game actually happened. Every move travels from its starting square to its landing square.

Colour is who moved

Each side has its own colour family, and every piece type has its own colour within it. Heavier pieces make bigger marks: a queen move carries about nine times the weight of a pawn move. Captures use the captured piece’s colour, and checks use the accent.

Same game, same picture

All the randomness is seeded by the moves themselves, so a game always paints the same picture in a given style. Remix gives you another take on it.

Numbered for filtering

Every style has a fixed code, group number first. Type 3 for a whole group, 2.4 for one style, a name like oil, or a list like 1.1, 5.3, 9. Star your favourites, and note that downloads (PDF and video) carry the code in their filename.
